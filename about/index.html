Our story

Built at the intersection of healthcare and remote work

Nessar Health exists because good administrative support shouldn't be the reason a practice falls behind.

Why we started Nessar Health

Nessar Health was founded by someone who has stood on both sides of this problem, with a background in pharmacology and hands-on experience supporting fintech and edtech teams remotely.

That combination made one thing clear: healthcare practices are drowning in administrative work that has nothing to do with patient care, while skilled, capable remote talent often struggles to find the right opportunity to prove itself.

Nessar Health is the bridge between those two realities: matching healthcare practices with Remote Healthcare Support Specialists who are trained, reliable, and genuinely invested in getting it right.

What we stand for
